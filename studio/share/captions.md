# Caption rules for the 11 AM share days

The routine writes `caption.txt` from these rules, then runs
`python3 tools/share_run.py caption-check W`, which enforces the mechanical ones.
These posts exist to be sent to someone, so the caption asks for the send first.

1. **Line 1 asks for the send**, under 110 characters, because Instagram cuts the
   caption there. Use the day's line, or one in the same spirit, never the same
   wording two weeks running:
   - Wednesday (thought): "Saw this and thought of you. Send it to the one who came to mind." /
     "Who came to mind first? Send this to them."
   - Thursday (person): "Send this to your person." /
     "For the one you would wait for. Send it to them."
   - Sunday (blessing): "A blessing for your week. Send it to someone you are praying for." /
     on a month's first Sunday, "Hello, October. A blessing for the month ahead."
     (with the month's name).
2. **Then one or two plain sentences**, warm, the way a friend would talk, naming
   what the verse is about. Do not retype the verse and do not preach.
   - Never say God picked a verse for the reader, and never treat a verse like a
     fortune or a promise it does not make.
   - The people in the photos are not real: never give them a name, a story, or
     words beyond the slide, and never describe the photo as "me" or "us".
   - Thursday: the conversation is the couple's, the verse is God's. Never present
     the conversation as Scripture.
3. **Then the reference** on its own line, ending with "(BSB)", for example
   `Isaiah 41:10 (BSB)`.
4. **Then one save line**, rotating: "Save this for the week ahead." /
   "Save it for a day you need it." / "Keep this one close."
5. **Sign-off line:** `🕯️ Chasten is a free Bible app. chasten.ai`
6. **Last line: three to five lowercase hashtags** and nothing else. Always
   `#bibleverse #faith`, plus one to three for the day: Wednesday
   `#encouragement #christiangirl`, Thursday `#christiancouple #godlylove`, Sunday
   `#blessing #sundayblessing`.

Voice: warm, reverent, plain. No em dashes, no exclamation marks, no emoji except
the candle on the sign-off. Nothing that scolds or pressures. Under 1,200 characters.
