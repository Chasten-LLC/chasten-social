# Caption rules for the 7 AM post

The routine writes `caption.txt` from these rules, then runs
`python3 tools/morning.py caption-check W`, which enforces the mechanical ones.

1. **Line 1 invites the interaction**, under 110 characters, because Instagram cuts
   the caption there. Use the day's line, or one in the same spirit, never the same
   wording two weeks running:
   - Monday (swipe to reveal): "Hold the dots and slide. Your verse for the week is in there." /
     "Slide to the end for this week's verse."
   - Tuesday (follow the line): "Which line did you follow? Tell us the number." /
     "Follow your line, then tell us where it landed."
   - Wednesday (zoom): "Keep swiping. You're closer than you think." /
     "Swipe all the way in."
   - Thursday and Saturday (reel): "Hold the screen. Which verse did you stop on?" /
     "Hold to stop, then tell us the verse you landed on."
   - Friday (sunrise): "Hold the dots and slide to watch the sun come up." /
     "Good morning. Hold the dots and slide."
   - Sunday (dove): "Follow the dove, then send this to someone you love." /
     "A blessing for your week. Follow the dove to the end."
2. **Then one or two plain sentences**, warm, the way a friend would talk, naming
   what the verse is about. Do not retype the verse and do not preach.
   - Never say God picked a verse for the reader, and never treat a verse like a
     fortune. On reel days say plainly that every one of these is true.
   - On Mondays the women in the photo are not real people: never give them a name,
     a story, or words they did not say, and never describe the photo as "me".
3. **Then the references** on one line, in slide order, ending with "(BSB)", for
   example `1 Peter 5:7, Matthew 11:28, Deuteronomy 31:8, Psalm 118:24 (BSB)`.
   On reel days, instead: `45 verses from the Berean Standard Bible (BSB)`.
4. **Then one share line**, rotating: "Send this to someone who needs it today." /
   "Save this for the week ahead." / "Share it with a friend who could use it."
5. **Sign-off line:** `🕯️ Chasten is a free Bible app. chasten.ai`
6. **Last line: three to five lowercase hashtags** and nothing else. Always
   `#bibleverse #faith`, plus one to three for the day:
   Monday `#christiangirl #jesuslovesyou`, Tuesday `#dailydevotional`, Wednesday
   `#encouragement`, Thursday and Saturday `#christianreels #bibleverses`, Friday
   `#goodmorning`, Sunday `#blessing #prayer`.

Voice: warm, reverent, plain. No em dashes, no exclamation marks, no emoji except
the candle on the sign-off. Nothing that scolds or pressures. Under 1,200 characters.
