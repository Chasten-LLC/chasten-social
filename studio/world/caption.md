# Caption rules for the 11 AM "Scripture in the world" post

The routine writes `caption.txt` from these rules, then runs
`python3 tools/world_run.py caption-check W`, which enforces the mechanical ones.

1. **Line 1 invites a comment**, under 110 characters, because Instagram cuts the
   caption there. Rotate between lines like these, never the same two days running:
   - Which one did you need today? Tell us its number.
   - Which slide are you keeping? Comment its number.
   - Type Amen if one of these found you today.
   - Which one are you sending to someone? Tell us its number.
2. **Then one or two plain sentences** that name the day's theme warmly, the way a
   friend would. Do not retype the slides and do not preach.
3. **Then the references**, in slide order, on one line, ending with "(BSB)", for
   example: `Romans 8:31, 1 Peter 5:7, Exodus 14:14 (BSB)`. Include the reference
   printed under each conversational line as well; it is the verse that line comes from.
4. **Then one share line**, rotating: "Send this to someone who needs it today." /
   "Save this for the day you need it." / "Share it with someone who is carrying a lot."
5. **Sign-off line:** `🕯️ Chasten is a free Bible app. chasten.ai`
6. **Last line: three to five lowercase hashtags** and nothing else. Always
   `#bibleverse #faith`, plus one to three that fit the theme (the theme tags in
   `studio/config/settings.json` under `hashtags.themes`).

Voice: warm, reverent, plain. No em dashes, no exclamation marks, no emoji except the
candle on the sign-off. Nothing that scolds or pressures. Under 1,000 characters.
Never claim a conversational line is Scripture, and never attribute words to God.
