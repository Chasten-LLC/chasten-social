Research from ElevenLabs' public pages, read 2026-11-17. **This is research, not legal advice.** Chasten has no lawyer before launch, so it records what the pages say, with dates, and where they leave a gap.

## The voices: fine for public posts, as far as the pages say

- **Commercial use.** The Addendum leaves a library voice's rights to the Terms. The Terms (1(c)) let a paid user use the Services "for commercial purposes", subject to the Prohibited Use Policy. Starter is paid. Downloaded Output may be used outside the Services (4(a)). No attribution is required anywhere in the voice pages read.
- **Who owns the clips.** Terms 4(c)(ii): "you retain all rights in and to your Output".
- **If a voice is withdrawn.** Addendum section 4: Outputs made before the notice period ends "will continue to exist and remain available for use thereafter", with no end date. Section 6: leaving the program does not undo licensing "up to the point of termination". No clause read lets the voice owner or ElevenLabs take back rights in Output already made or demand that published posts come down. ElevenLabs can remove a voice model and content held on its own service (8; Terms 4(j)), not copies already downloaded.
- **So for the reels:** the 90 clips already exist and stay usable if any of the four voices leaves the library. What a withdrawal would stop is NEW clips with that voice, for a later batch.
- **Notice period.** The owner sets 30 days to 2 years and cannot shorten it. Whether using a voice by id through the API locks in that notice period is not clear: the Addendum protects users who "added" the voice, while the help page says "saved or used it previously". Adding the four voices to My Voices would remove the doubt. That changes the account, so it is Ric's call.
- **Content rules that touch us.** The Prohibited Use Policy bars deceptive ads and "falsely implying endorsement". The reels never present the voice as a person speaking for Chasten. Religious content is not mentioned.

## The theme music: a real question

The current Music Terms (Exhibit A) give Starter: "All online and offline commercial use permitted, except film, TV, radio, and Studio Games", no attribution, but **eligibility "For Individual Use Only"**. The plans that allow a business are listed as Scale (under 10 employees) and Business (under 50). Section 1.4 says being on a plan you are not eligible for "may result in suspension or termination of your account or loss of Output". Section 1.5 says Output keeps the terms of the plan in force when it was made, so upgrading later would not, by itself, change a track made on Starter.

This was not in our 2026-10-08 notes, which read the Starter column's media rights but not its eligibility row. It touches every ElevenLabs track Chasten LLC posts, not only this theme: the strings bed, the dove's harp and the wing sounds in the 7 AM posts too.

Ways out, for Ric to choose:

1. Move the ElevenLabs account to a plan that names businesses, then make the theme again on it (same prompt, his ear picks again). Clearest; costs a higher plan.
2. Use a bed from a library whose license names business use on social media (the repo's `studio/audio/tracks.json` holds some already), and keep the ElevenLabs theme off public posts.
3. Ask ElevenLabs support whether a one-person LLC on Starter counts as "individual use".

Recommendation: 3 first, because it costs nothing and an answer in writing settles it. Meanwhile the routine and its checks do not depend on which bed it uses.
