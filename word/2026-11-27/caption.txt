The Hebrew word behind “loving devotion” in Psalm 136:1.

חֶסֶד (chesed): “loyal love, mercy.” The same word is “mercy” in Micah 6:8: “to love mercy.”

Psalm 136:1 (BSB): Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good. His loving devotion endures forever.

Said the way it is spoken in Israel today: KHEH-sed. Hebrew reads right to left.
