The Greek word behind “Love” in Mark 12:30.

ἀγαπάω (agapaō): “to love.” The same word gives “My beloved,” God’s name for a people once unloved (Romans 9:25).

Mark 12:30 (BSB): Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind and with all your strength.’

Said the way Greeks say it today: ah-ghah-PAH-oh.
