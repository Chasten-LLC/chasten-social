The Greek word behind “Peace” in John 14:27.

εἰρήνη (eirēnē): “peace, safety.” It is safety too: in Luke 11:21 a guarded house’s goods “are secure,” literally at peace.

John 14:27 (BSB): Peace I leave with you; My peace I give to you. I do not give to you as the world gives. Do not let your hearts be troubled; do not be afraid.

Said the way Greeks say it today: ee-REE-nee.
