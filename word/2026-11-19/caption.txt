The Greek word behind “power” in 2 Timothy 1:7.

δύναμις (dynamis): “power, ability.” The same word is “miracles” in Matthew 13:58: works of power.

2 Timothy 1:7 (BSB): For God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power, love, and self-control.

Said the way Greeks say it today: THEE-nah-mees.
