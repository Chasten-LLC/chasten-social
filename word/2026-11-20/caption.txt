The Hebrew word behind “good” in Psalm 34:8.

טוֹב (tov): “good, pleasing.” It covers every kind of good. In Genesis 6:2 the same word is “beautiful.”

Psalm 34:8 (BSB): Taste and see that the LORD is good; blessed is the man who takes refuge in Him!

Said the way it is spoken in Israel today: tohv. Hebrew reads right to left.
