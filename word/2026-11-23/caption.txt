The Hebrew word behind “justice” in Amos 5:24.

מִשְׁפָּט (mishpat): “justice, judgment.” It is also a ruling. Exodus 21:1 calls God’s laws “the ordinances” with this word.

Amos 5:24 (BSB): But let justice roll on like a river, and righteousness like an ever-flowing stream.

Said the way it is spoken in Israel today: meesh-PAHT. Hebrew reads right to left.
