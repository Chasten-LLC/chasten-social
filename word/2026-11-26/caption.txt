The Greek word behind “overcome” in John 1:5.

καταλαμβάνω (katalambanō): “to take hold of.” It can also mean to understand, so the King James reads “comprehended.”

John 1:5 (BSB): The Light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it.

Said the way Greeks say it today: kah-tah-lahm-VAH-no.
