The Greek word behind “wisdom” in James 1:5.

σοφία (sophia): “wisdom.” It can grow: the boy Jesus “grew in wisdom and stature” (Luke 2:52).

James 1:5 (BSB): Now if any of you lacks wisdom, he should ask God, who gives generously to all without finding fault, and it will be given to him.

Said the way Greeks say it today: so-FEE-ah.
