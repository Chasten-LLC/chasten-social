The Greek word behind “Cast” in 1 Peter 5:7.

ἐπιῤῥίπτω (epirrhiptō): “to throw upon.” Luke uses it of the cloaks thrown over the colt Jesus rode (Luke 19:35).

1 Peter 5:7 (BSB): Cast all your anxiety on Him, because He cares for you.

Said the way Greeks say it today: eh-pee-REEP-toh.
