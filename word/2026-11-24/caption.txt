The Hebrew word behind “praise” in Psalm 139:14.

יָדָה (yadah): “to give thanks, to praise.” The same word is “Give thanks” in Psalm 136:1.

Psalm 139:14 (BSB): I praise You, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made. Marvelous are Your works, and I know this very well.

Said the way it is spoken in Israel today: yah-DAH. Hebrew reads right to left.
