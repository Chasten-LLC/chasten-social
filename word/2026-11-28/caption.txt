The Greek word behind “gives me strength” in Philippians 4:13.

ἐνδυναμόω (endynamoō): “to make strong.” Paul gives it as a command in Ephesians 6:10: “be strong in the Lord.”

Philippians 4:13 (BSB): I can do all things through Christ who gives me strength.

Said the way Greeks say it today: en-thee-nah-MOH-oh.
